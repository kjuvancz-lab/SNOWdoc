TMF APIs for TMT
®

Get the details of ServiceNow implementation of TM Forum (TMF) APIs for
Telecommunications, Media, and Technology (TMT) products.
®

ServiceNow supports multiple TM Forum (TMF) Open APIs through its telecommunications
industry solutions, such as Telecommunications Service Management (TSM) and Technology
Provider Service Management (TPSM). These APIs enable standardized integrations and help
ensure seamless interoperability across Business Support Systems (BSS) and Operations
Support Systems (OSS).
