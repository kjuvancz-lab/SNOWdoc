Managing teams and incidents using My Team applet
Easily access and get insights into the work of the groups you are managing.
With the My Team applet, as a group manager you can:
• Easily access unassigned incidents from your group and quickly triage work
• Act upon time-off requests submitted by your team
• Review any On-call conflicts or gaps in a schedule and plan for appropriate coverage
• Access dashboards to gain insights like MTTR, CSAT, and so on for your groups.

My team dashboard
As a group manager, with the My team dashboard, gain insights into the data like incidents at
risk, breached incidents or overdue changes.
Tap on the sections to process the incidents.

Manage upcoming shifts with On-Call Scheduling
As an On-Call Scheduling manager, view your team's schedule and perform activities like
approving time-off requests and providing coverage.

Before you begin

Role required: rota_manager

Procedure
1. Open the ITSM Mobile Agent app and tap My team.
2. On the On-call Schedule tab, tap Upcoming shifts.
All upcoming shifts for the current month are displayed. The shifts are grouped based on the
type of events. One-day events are displayed at the top, followed by multi-day events and
short-duration events that are sorted based on the time that the event was created.
3. Tap the shift card to view shift details.
4. Tap Request time-off and provide the following details:
◦ Group
◦ Member
◦ Start date
◦ End date
◦ Proposed coverage
◦ Notes
5. Tap Submit.
6. Tap Provide Coverage and provide the following details:
◦ Start date
◦ End date
◦ Coverage
7. Tap Submit.
8. In Upcoming shifts, swipe the shift card to the left and tap Request time-off to go to the
Request time-off form.
9. In Upcoming shifts, swipe the shift card to the left and tap Provide Coverage to go to the
Provide Coverage form.

A list of time-off requests is displayed. The list is divided into pending and completed requests.
3. Tap Approve or Reject the time-off requests that you've received.

Manage gaps and conflicts with On-Call Scheduling
Resolve all gaps and conflicts for the shifts in your group.

Before you begin

Role required: rota_manager

Procedure
1. Open the ITSM Mobile Agent app and tap My team.
2. On the On-call Schedule tab, tap Gaps and conflicts.
A list of all the gaps and conflicts for the shifts in your group is displayed.
3. Tap any day of the month to view all gaps and conflicts for the shifts on that day.
4. Tap a shift card to view gaps and conflicts for the shift.
5. Swipe a shift card to the left and tap Provide coverage to provide or replace coverage, delete
coverage, and manage a shift.
6. On the Gaps and conflicts form, tap Provide coverage to manage the coverage for a gap in the
shift.

Performance metrics
Access the performance data of your team directly from your mobile with the ITSM Mobile Agent
app.
As a group manager, with the My team dashboard, gain insights into the work of the groups you
are managing by accessing metrics like MTTR (Mean Time To Resolution), CSAT, breached SLAs
and so on.
Also view the graphical representation of the incidents raised in a week.
