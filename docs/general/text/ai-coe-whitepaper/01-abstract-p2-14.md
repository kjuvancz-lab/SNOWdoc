Abstract

This whitepaper explores how a AI Center of Excellence and Innovation (CoEI) - the organizational construct designed
to maximize the strategic value of ServiceNow - can be transformed into an AI-driven engine of continuous
innovation - the so-called AI CoEI.
As enterprises face accelerating change, the convergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and IT Agents is reshaping how
work is designed, executed, and scaled. These capabilities move organizations beyond reactive operations, enabling
predictive insights, autonomous process orchestration, and seamless cross-system integration.
Through a structured three-stage maturity model - Foundational, Advanced, and Strategic - this paper provides a
clear roadmap for integrating AI into every layer of the CoEI. It offers practical guidance on governance, stakeholder
alignment, and technology adoption, ensuring AI becomes an embedded, enduring capability rather than a
standalone initiative.
Readers will gain actionable insights on how to align human expertise with intelligent automation, create measurable
business impact, and maintain a competitive edge in a rapidly evolving digital landscape.
The message is clear: organizations that operationalize AI within their CoEI will not only keep pace with change - but
they will also define the future of work.

2 Introduction

Whether you're just starting to explore ServiceNow - gaining an understanding of its core capabilities and seeking a
roadmap to begin - or you've already established a solid platform foundation and are ready to move beyond stability
into true strategic innovation, this whitepaper is for you. It serves both those building a Center of Excellence and
Innovation (CoEI) from the ground up and those looking to elevate an established CoEI into a high-impact, AI-driven
growth engine.

In today's digital economy, simply "keeping the lights on" is no longer enough. Transformation is no longer a strategic
option--it's a baseline requirement. The rapid emergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and IT Agents is redefining
how organizations create value, turning static workflows into dynamic, autonomous, and predictive operations. At
the same time, people and organizations are reimagining their roles in this new landscape - leveraging AI to focus
on higher-value, creative, and strategic work rather than routine tasks. These capabilities are not add-ons; they are
the next evolution of enterprise platforms. The question is no longer whether to adopt AI, but how quickly and
effectively you can embed it into your operating model. That's exactly where a ServiceNow CoEI - designed to
integrate AI and orchestrate intelligent agents - becomes your competitive advantage.

This whitepaper challenges the status quo, showing you how to move beyond incremental change into sustained,
AI-enabled innovation, strategic value delivery, and organizational maturity.

2.1 Who is this Whitepaper For?

This whitepaper is designed for leaders who shape and steward their organization's digital and AI-enabled
transformation. It provides strategic guidance for CXOs seeking to align technology investments with business value
and long-term enterprise priorities.

Executive Sponsors will find clear direction on how to champion change, secure alignment, and remove barriers
across the organization.

Platform Owners and Platform Architects can leverage this framework to scale ServiceNow with strong governance,
architectural consistency, and intelligent automation.

Transformation Leads will gain practical insights to drive cross-functional collaboration, accelerate delivery, and
ensure measurable outcomes.

Business Owners will understand how to harness the platform to improve processes, empower teams, and
contribute directly to organizational growth.

2.2 What's in this Whitepaper

Inside, you'll find a framework - developed by experienced practitioners - that takes you from foundational setup to
advanced optimization and, ultimately, a strategic state of continuous, AI-powered innovation. The AI CoEI evolution
is presented in four maturity steps:

     · Foundational Elements - For customers beginning their journey without dedicated partner or ServiceNow
          support, establishing governance, platform stability, and identifying early AI opportunities.

     · Advanced Elements - For organizations leveraging ServiceNow Impact to evolve their CoEI, embedding AI
          analytics, predictive insights, and IT Agents to accelerate delivery and decision-making.

     · Strategic Elements - For enterprises ready to operate an innovation-driven, AI-orchestrated CoEI where
          humans and intelligent agents work in tandem to transform business models at scale.

3 Foundational Elements - your start to implement your AI CoEI

Embarking on a ServiceNow implementation involves more than simply deploying a new technology platform; it
requires a shift in how an organization manages processes, resources, and its own culture. At the heart of this
transformation lies a robust AI CoEI. By focusing on four foundational pillars - Goals, Processes, People, and
Innovation - organizations can accelerate the value they derive from ServiceNow and position themselves for
sustained growth.

Figure 3: ServiceNow AI CoEI Framework Foundation

3.1 Goals
Clear, measurable goals are the key drivers of any successful ServiceNow journey. Without explicit objectives and
tangible metrics, even the most innovative implementations risk losing direction and failing to deliver results that
resonate with the broader business.
Alignment with Business Needs: Goals that map directly to organizational priorities ensure that ServiceNow
programs remain relevant and gain the buy-in of executive stakeholders. By tying initiatives to cost savings, customer
experience improvements, or other recognized business value, an AI CoEI can justify and protect its investments
over time.
Motivation and Accountability: When goals are specific and measurable, project teams feel a heightened sense of
purpose. This clarity fosters a results-oriented mindset, driving better collaboration and higher-quality deliverables.
Scaling and Future-readiness: As your ServiceNow footprint grows, goals serve as a compass. Whether you're
optimizing IT services, expanding into HR workflows, or developing custom apps, clear targets keep each new project
aligned with your overarching strategic direction.

Actionable Recommendations

3.2 Processes
Robust, well-defined processes form the structural backbone of a CoEI. By streamlining workflows and adopting
strong governance mechanisms, organizations can establish a consistent framework for delivery and continuous
improvement.
Efficiency and Consistency: Standardized processes minimize bottlenecks and reduce errors. This consistency is
especially crucial when your CoEI scales to multiple lines of business or geographic locations.
Governance and Risk Management: Thoughtful process design and governance structures (e.g., change approval
boards and role-based permissions) help mitigate risks, ensure compliance, and maintain the integrity of ServiceNow
instances.
Adaptability to Change: As new ServiceNow capabilities and business needs emerge, well-documented and flexible
processes make it easier to pivot quickly. This agility can offer a significant competitive advantage in rapidly changing
markets.

Actionable Recommendations
  Document Current and Future Workflows: Begin with an in-depth analysis of existing workflows, identifying
  inefficiencies and potential automation opportunities. Then, design a roadmap that progressively refines these
  processes.
  Leverage Standard Methodologies: Employ industry best practices like ITIL2 for IT Service Management or other
  relevant frameworks. Where possible, use ServiceNow's out-of-the-box functionality to expedite implementation
  and minimize custom development.
  Implement a Strong Governance Model: Define escalation paths, change management protocols, and periodic
  audits. This not only keeps the platform secure and compliant but also sets a high standard of operational
  excellence.

3.3 People

No matter how sophisticated your goals or processes are, success ultimately depends on the people who bring them
to life. By empowering individuals and fostering collaboration, a CoEI can amplify the impact of the ServiceNow
platform.

Skill Development and Retention: Investing in targeted training ensures your team remains capable of designing
and maintaining advanced ServiceNow solutions. This reduces reliance on external consultants and builds lasting
internal expertise.

Cross-Functional Collaboration: ServiceNow implementations touch multiple departments-IT, HR, finance, customer
service, and beyond. Encouraging diverse stakeholders to collaborate fosters a holistic perspective that can lead to
more innovative solutions.

Leadership and Culture: Executive sponsorship and middle management support are vital. A culture that rewards
creativity and continuous learning will attract top talent and encourage fresh ideas.

Actionable Recommendations

  Clearly Define Roles and Responsibilities: Document key responsibilities for every member involved in the
  ServiceNow program - from business analysts to technical leads-so that each person understands how they
  contribute to overarching goals.

  Provide Regular Training and Certification: ServiceNow offers a range of certifications and learning paths.
  Encourage team members to pursue these opportunities and set aside budget and time for professional
  development.

  Champion Continuous Knowledge-Sharing: Host regular lunch-and-learn sessions, internal demos, and
  community forums. This helps cultivate a sense of ownership and maintains a collective understanding of best
  practices.

3.4 Innovation

Innovation is what propels a CoEI beyond routine platform maintenance into a strategic driver of transformative
change. Harnessing new ideas and staying on top of evolving trends ensures that ServiceNow remains a key
differentiator in your organization's technology landscape.

Continuous Improvement Mindset: Beyond the initial implementation, there should be an ongoing pursuit of
enhancements that improve speed, quality, and user experience. Even small improvements can unlock significant
value over time.

Experimentation and Prototyping: A structured approach to testing ideas-through prototypes, pilots, or proofs of
concept - helps teams fail fast, learn quickly, and refine solutions before large-scale rollouts.

Staying Ahead of the Curve: ServiceNow regularly introduces new features, such as AI-driven chatbots or advanced
analytics. By actively exploring emerging capabilities, a CoEI can position itself as a forward-thinking hub of
innovation within the enterprise.

Actionable Recommendations

  Establish an Idea and Demand Pipeline: Create a channel for employees to suggest new use cases or refinements.
  Evaluate these ideas based on factors like feasibility, impact, and alignment with organizational goals - Best
  Practice is to leverage Strategic Portfolio Management (SPM).
