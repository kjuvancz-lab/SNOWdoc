8 The Financial Fuel for ServiceNow AI CoEIs: A Blueprint for Success

Establishing an AI CoEI is no longer a luxury - it is a prerequisite for organizations aiming to thrive in an era where
digital agility drives market leadership. Research by McKinsey & Company shows that organizations that align
investment decisions early with digital and business transformation objectives are significantly more likely to capture
and sustain value from their transformation efforts.13 In the ServiceNow ecosystem, a well-funded AI CoEI acts as
the engine that powers streamlined processes, rapid innovation, and tangible business impact. Even the most
advanced "engine," however, will stall if not fueled by a solid and strategic financial framework.
The following sections outline why funding matters and how to devise a robust, transparent financial model that
optimally supports your ServiceNow AI CoEI. We also provide key actions and real-world customer insights to guide
the successful deployment of this model across your organization.

8.1 Why Funding matters

A solid funding strategy is fundamental to long-term success. It ensures that your AI CoEI can:
 Sustain Operations and Attract Talent: Without adequate funding, even the brightest minds can't innovate.

    Financial backing secures top talent and fosters a culture of experimentation.
 Align with Strategic Goals: Funding isn't just about budget allocation; it is about ensuring every dollar spent

    aligns with the organization's overarching objectives.
 Deliver Tangible Business Value: A well-funded AI CoEI fully exploits the ServiceNow platform - driving

    operational efficiency, transforming workflows, and maximizing ROI.

"Innovation must be financed. Without a strategic approach to funding, even the most groundbreaking ideas remain
stuck"14

10 Crafting the Dream Team: The Secret Sauce to a Thriving

     "My model for business is The Beatles. They were four guys who kept each other's kind of negative tendencies
     in check. They balanced each other, and the total was greater than the sum of the parts. That's how I see
     business: great things in business are never done by one person, they're done by a team of people."20

                                                                                                                           (Nasdaq 2015)
Establishing a well-structured ServiceNow AI CoEI is critical for organizations aiming to fully leverage digital- and
business transformation. The right team - comprising clear roles, responsibilities, and synergies - is the backbone of
any successful AI CoEI. Dedicated internal roles foster a deep understanding of both the organization's culture and
its strategic objectives, enabling ServiceNow capabilities to flourish under guided leadership and effective
governance.

10.1 Why are specific roles crucial?
     Specific roles within an AI CoEI ensure that governance frameworks are not only enacted but also maintained
     over time. Internal team members bring both stability and intimate organizational knowledge, making them
     uniquely positioned to:
          · Align Technology and Strategy: Guarantee that every ServiceNow initiative supports long-term
                business objectives.
          · Provide Proactive Platform Management: Tackle budgetary and integration issues before they
                escalate.
          · Enable clear Accountability: Avoid role duplication and confusion, allowing for focused resource
                allocation.

11.4 Summary and Outlook

Strategic partner management is a powerful lever for unlocking the full potential of your ServiceNow CoEI. By

organizational culture to maintain both flexibility and coherence.
