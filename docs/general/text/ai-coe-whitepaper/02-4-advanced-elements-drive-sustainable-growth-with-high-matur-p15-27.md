5 Shaping the Future: The Power of Vision in Driving ServiceNow's
    Strategic Transformation

In today's digital era, transformation is not a luxury but an imperative. Establishing a clear and compelling vision for
the ServiceNow platform within your organization's AI CoEI forms the foundation for success. As Stephen R. Covey
aptly put it, "Begin with the end in mind."3 A well-defined vision serves as the North Star, steering organizations
through the intricate pathways of digital and business transformation with clarity and purpose.

5.1 Why a vision for the ServiceNow Platform is essential
A vision articulates the desired future state and provides a shared goal for the entire organization. It aligns
stakeholders, inspires innovation, and drives excellence. A robust vision is indispensable for:

           Strategic Alignment: Ensuring all initiatives resonate with the organization's broader objectives.
           Innovation and Growth: Positioning the platform as a scalable and sustainable driver of digital

                excellence.
           Value Proposition Clarity: Helping customers, employees, and stakeholders understand the purpose

                and benefits of the platform.
As Peter Drucker famously stated, "The best way to predict the future is to create it."4 Crafting a vision not only
defines the roadmap but also strengthens the organization's ability to adapt to dynamic market conditions.

5.2 How to craft an impactful vision
The process of crafting a vision is as crucial as the vision itself. It requires inclusivity, insight, and actionable planning.
Key steps include, as can be seen in Figure 5:
Stakeholder Collaboration:

passive support is not enough.
Incorporate insights from diverse stakeholders, including customers, employees, and market analysts.

Leverage customer feedback and market trends to root the vision while retaining ambition.

6 Crafting a Comprehensive Strategy for Sustainable Success with
    ServiceNow

"Strategy without tactics is the slowest route to victory. Tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat"6
                                                                                                                                    Sun Tzu

In today's dynamic business environment, executing on a clear, agile strategy is critical for maximizing the value of
any digital- and business transformation initiative, including those on the ServiceNow platform. This section outlines
why a ServiceNow strategy is vital, how to formulate it, and what drives it toward sustained success. In the words of
management guru Peter Drucker, "Plans are only good intentions unless they immediately degenerate into hard
work."7

6.1 Why do we need a strategy for the ServiceNow Platform?
A robust strategy not only illuminates your organization's destination - it prescribes the concrete steps to get there.
In aligning technological investments with broader business objectives, you ensure that every ServiceNow initiative
directly advances the organization's overarching goals. This alignment is critical for:

   Driving Efficiency and Effectiveness: Driving Efficiency and Effectiveness: A clear strategy reduces duplicated
    efforts, streamlines workflows, and ensures resources are used optimally. It also gives teams a shared direction,
    enabling faster decision-making and filtering out low-value activities that waste time and resources.

   Enhancing Stakeholder Engagement: Stakeholders gain visibility into how each strategic priority supports the
    broader mission, boosting commitment and support.

   Maintaining Competitive Edge: By actively anticipating and responding to market shifts, a well-crafted strategy
    embeds agility and fosters a culture of innovation.

   Ensuring Transparency: A shared strategic vision helps break down silos, clarifying direction and progress for all
    relevant parties.

According to a McKinsey Global Survey, fewer than 30% of organizations report success with their digital initiatives,
underscoring the need for a well-defined strategic approach8. A thoughtful strategy is precisely the link between
intention and disciplined execution.

6.3 What drives the Strategy, and what is it good for?

   Vision Realization: Strategy transforms aspirational goals into operational reality, ensuring the organization
      remains laser-focused on customer satisfaction and digital excellence.

   Structured Execution: By establishing a strategic map, all teams know what tasks and deliverables to prioritize,
      reducing confusion and conflicting agendas.

   Long-Term Sustainability: A cohesive strategy underpins scalable growth and continuous improvement, vital
      for staying relevant in an ever-evolving market.

   Risk Mitigation: A well-planned strategy helps you anticipate obstacles, pivot proactively, and measure
      progress effectively, lowering the chance of costly missteps.

This strategic approach sets the stage for a detailed roadmap - one that transitions you from planning to execution
with minimal friction. "Structure follows strategy," observed historian and management theorist Alfred D.
Chandler11, highlighting that organizational effectiveness hinges on aligning business structures and processes with
a clearly defined plan.

7 What Are the Benefits of a Well-Constructed ServiceNow
    Roadmap?

"The best technology road maps deliver vital advantages to companies--helping them to... satisfy customers'
changing needs, boost returns on technology investments... and enhance transparency into where they are going"12

                                                                                                          (McKinsey & Company 2017)
A well-structured ServiceNow roadmap serves as both a compass and a blueprint, guiding your organization toward
tangible outcomes while ensuring each initiative remains strategically aligned with overall business goals.

7.1 The Value Proposition of a Strategic Roadmap
     · Clear Direction and Execution Plan: A roadmap offers explicit timelines and well-defined milestones,
          enabling stakeholders to track progress, allocate resources effectively, and adapt as needed. This clarity
          reduces uncertainty and fosters collaboration across departments.
     · Scalability and Sustainability: By mapping out long-term objectives and incrementally building capabilities,
          your ServiceNow roadmap helps ensure that all initiatives remain scalable and sustainable. This structured
          approach supports ongoing growth and continuous improvement throughout the organization.
     · Proactive Change Management: Roadmaps that undergo regular updates and revisions keep pace with
          evolving market trends and internal dynamics. This not only preserves relevance in a rapidly shifting
          environment but also demonstrates adaptability - a key trust factor for both leadership and end users.
     · Increased Stakeholder Confidence: A solid roadmap, backed by data-driven justifications and transparent
          timelines, instills confidence among executives, employees, and customers. By showcasing a proactive
          approach to managing digital- and business transformation, you pave the way for broader buy-in and
          successful adoption of new capabilities.
