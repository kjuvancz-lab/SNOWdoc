18 The Skilled Workforce Dilemma: Is Your CoEI Doomed Before It
    Begins?

While not phrased identically, analysts emphasize that technology alone doesn't shape the future of work -- human
skills and adaptability remain central (see The Future Of Work Isn't About Technology -- It's About People, Forbes,
May 2021).30

In an era where digital- and business transformation sets the tempo of competition, organizations are racing to
establish an AI CoEIs. These hubs promise to streamline processes and fuel innovation, yet one glaring issue is often
overlooked: the scarcity of a skilled workforce. Without the right talent, even the most visionary AI CoEI becomes
little more than a symbolic gesture. This article addresses the human element critical to a thriving AI CoEI, shedding
light on the global skills gap, real-world success stories, and the forward-thinking strategies needed to cultivate and
retain top talent.

18.1 The Unseen Barrier: Availability of Skilled Workforce

Technological investments and sizable budgets alone do not guarantee AI CoEI success. People - skilled, innovative,
and adaptable - are the indispensable drivers of progress. However, the global skills gap is intensifying:

Great Resignation (2021-2022): A workforce upheaval in which millions left their jobs seeking better opportunities
or work-life balance.31

Korn Ferry Report: Predicts a global talent shortage of 85 million people by 2030, risking $8.5 trillion in lost
revenue.32

The implications for AI CoEIs are strong. Failing to secure skilled professionals jeopardizes not only short-term
goals but also the organization's long-term competitiveness. This skills shortage is an iceberg: the visible gap is
minor compared to the underlying systemic challenges.

19 Conclusion: Why your CoEI Holds the Key to Future Success

"Innovation distinguishes between a leader and a follower."

                                                              Steve Jobs 44

As markets evolve at breakneck speed, AI Centers of Excellence and Innovation have become imperative for
organizations aiming to lead rather than lag. The pressing question is whether your AI CoEI is merely an isolated
"think-tank" or a dynamic engine capable of operationalizing emerging technologies - from AI and IoT to predictive
analytics and automation. This article examines key industry trends, highlighting how a well-structured AI CoEI on
the ServiceNow platform can turn digital complexity into tangible, competitive advantage.

19.1 Industry Trends Shaping the Future

Today's AI CoEI must deliver more than experimentation; it must fuse technology investments with concrete,
bottom-line objectives. Companies that effectively align their AI CoEIs with organizational strategies are rewriting
sector benchmarks, while those clinging to outdated models risk obsolescence.

     1. Rapid Technological Advancement

           AI, blockchain, IoT, and cloud-native solutions are redefining entire industries at once.

           Forward-looking AI CoEIs operationalize these innovations - transforming them from pilot concepts
                into enterprise-wide engines of value.

     2. Customer-Centric Expectations

           Consumers increasingly demand personalized experiences and seamless service across channels.

           An AI CoEI can orchestrate technologies (e.g., chatbots, predictive analytics) to deliver frictionless
                journeys and real-time engagement.

     3. Fierce Competitive Pressures

           Boards demand faster innovation cycles and demonstrable ROI.

AI CoEIs that operate as strategic powerhouses rather than hobbyist labs compress time-to-market and keep
organizations ahead of rivals.
